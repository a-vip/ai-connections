❓ How to Play AI Connections

Objective: Find 4 groups of 4 items that share an AI-related link without making 4 mistakes.

Difficulty Color Coding:
  • 🟨 Yellow: Straightforward / Core AI & Policy concepts
  • 🟩 Green: Intermediate / Technical & Operational taxonomy
  • 🟦 Blue: Advanced / Treaties, Statutes, Caselaw & Frameworks
  • 🟪 Purple: Tricky / Wordplay, Acronyms, Lateral Associations

Beware of Red Herrings: Words that look like they belong to multiple groups have exactly one valid 4-group solution!

Intelligence Dossiers: Once a category is solved, click its card at any time to open its full legal citation, executive summary, and policy analysis.

Keyboard Shortcuts: Space or Enter to select, Backspace or Esc to deselect, S to shuffle.
